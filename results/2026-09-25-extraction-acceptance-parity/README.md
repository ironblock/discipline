# Extraction acceptance inverts, re-fired

The parity fire of `results/2026-08-10-extraction-acceptance-inverts`, pre-registered and ratified on #115: gate 1's first real customer, a re-firing compared with a band.

## Observation

The archived row found that a small extractor answering a self-contained ask off the accelerator accepts an order of magnitude more grounded facts than the large model extracting off the warm session prefix. It was fired once, on 2026-08-10, on a substrate instance no capture pinned. The row reports point estimates and no interval.

## Hypothesis

Re-fired on today's declared substrate, under the configuration the archived row declares, the extraction-seat bakeoff reproduces the archived row's result. Seat B is the small extractor answering a self-contained ask off the accelerator. Seat A is the large model extracting off the warm session prefix. Seat B has the larger deduped gate-accept rate, by an amount within the band derived from the archived row's committed tallies.

## Test

The pre-registration is #115's, ratified by the maintainer's answers posted 2026-09-25 (UTC), and is committed here as `pre-registration.json` and `decision-rule.toml`.

- **The band.** `band.py` derives it from the archived row's committed tallies. It pairs the 31 extraction forks by (turn, step) and runs a percentile bootstrap: 10,000 resamples, seed 20260810, level 0.95. The result is `band.json`, [0.024877, 0.21789] around 0.091469. It is stated as derived by `band.py`, never as a value the archived row reported.
- **The fire.** Seats A and B were re-fired once each on 2026-09-25, A then B, through the harness at `0e14292`, rebuilt. The declared environment routed seat B's extraction lane, and only its extraction lane, to the CPU substrate.
- **The box record.** The window's checks are recorded one field per command in `box.json`, with their raw outputs under `window/`: the verification script, the fingerprint check and the canary, before the first fork and after the last, and the seat-B canary before.
- **The word.** `apply.py`, the ratified applier, asks its unadjudicated checks (box, logs, routing, forks, offered) before it compares the effect with the band. The effect is seat B's deduped gate-accept rate minus seat A's, from integer tallies read through the archived row's own `grade.py`.

Re-run: `bash recompute.sh` checks every consumed artefact's digest, re-derives `band.json` byte for byte, re-runs the archived grader over the re-fired seats against `report.json` field by field, and re-derives `verdict.json` byte for byte.

## Results

Every unadjudicated check passed.

| | offered | accepted, deduped |
| --- | --- | --- |
| seat A, re-fired | 912 | 26 |
| seat B, re-fired | 1590 | 186 |

The re-fired effect is 0.088472, inside the band [0.024877, 0.21789]. The archived effect, derived from its tallies, is 0.091469.

Beside it, read by nothing:

- **Warmth.** The grader's behavioural byte-identity track held: both re-fired seats' 72 warm calls were within −114..0 tokens of the drive's recorded cache, as in the archive.
- **The runaway forks.** Six of seat B's 31 extraction forks ran to the 4,096-token limit, offering 964 of its 1,590 facts, against five offering 930 in the archive. None of seat A's did, against one in the archive.
- **The "different set" half.** None of seat A's 25 accepted items long enough to compare is contained in seat B's, as in the archive's 0 of 18.
- **The grader's full tables** are `report.json`.

## Conclusion

Supported, and at the weight the pre-registration set: the inversion held, at a size close to the archived one, on a declared substrate whose original instance was never measured. The band admits much less than that closeness suggests. This is a re-fire of a mechanism the research program has since retired, so it reproduces a result about a lane that no longer runs.

What is still unknown is the same as the archived row's: whether what either seat accepts is true of the mechanism.
